ABOUT

Not a fan club.
A place for people who use it.

Each meetup, a few members bring a laptop and show how they use AI at work and at home, including what went wrong. Topics come from the people who attend. You don't need to be an expert. Notes from your first month are welcome too.

  1. 01Accurate information about AI
  2. 02AI governance and safe use
  3. 03Hands-on experience, good and bad
  4. 04Getting to know the Anthropic ecosystem

For people who already use Claude or want to, people who want to use AI but don't know where to start, and people putting AI to work.
